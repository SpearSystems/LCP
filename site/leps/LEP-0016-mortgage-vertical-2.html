LEP-0016 — mortgage vertical 2.0.0: whole-unit applicant amounts, a declared currency, and side-by-side vertical versions

  • Title: mortgage vertical 2.0.0: whole-unit applicant amounts with a declared amount_currency, bounded values, and a rule for receivers that serve two versions of one vertical at once
  • Author(s): LCP maintainers
  • Date: 2026-11-19
  • Status: Implemented — shipped in v1.4.0 (PR #251, released 2026-11-19); Accepted 2026-11-19 (maintainer; v1.4 decision record (docs/DECISIONS-v1.4.md) D1–D4: review window and independent review waived).

Decision (maintainer, 2026-11-19)

Accepted under D1–D4 (docs/DECISIONS-v1.4.md) of the v1.4 decision record. The review window closed early with no comments, and no reviewer independent of the author took part (D1, D2). No PII class changes, so no security/privacy condition applies beyond the v1.3 record.

Conditions:

  • mortgage 1.0.0 stays served side by side for the 180-day window, frozen at verticals/archive/mortgage-1.0.0.json.
  • The SDKs carry both versions in their schema bundles and validate by attributes.schema_version. No SDK has typed per-vertical builders, so "1.0.0 builder deprecated" is carried as deprecated: true on the archived schema (D4 scope notes).
  • Any comment with new evidence on #258 reopens this record. Re-evaluation: 2027-05-19.

Problem statement and evidence

Mortgage amounts have no currency. verticals/mortgage.json 1.0.0 declares four applicant-declared amounts:

  • loan_amount_cents
  • property_value_cents
  • annual_income_cents
  • cash_out_amount_cents

They are minor units of a currency the schema never names, so a GBP or AUD mortgage lead is indistinguishable from a USD one.

They have no upper bound. Each is integer with minimum: 0 only. A value multiplied by 100 twice, the defect LEP-0014 records for personal_loans, is accepted without complaint.

They are typed by applicants in whole units. These are form answers, not platform-priced money. LEP-0014 sets the authoring rule:

  • applicant-declared amounts are whole units of a declared amount_currency;
  • machine-produced money stays integer _cents.

Once LEP-0014 lands, mortgage is the only vertical that breaks that rule. No-code form tools that cannot multiply a field (LEP-0014, evidence) are used for mortgage forms too.

Receivers cannot serve two versions of a vertical. Capabilities already advertise verticals[{id, schema_version}] (spec/05 §capabilities), so a receiver can list mortgage 1.0.0 and 2.0.0. The spec says nothing about validating both. The reference platform keys vertical validators by id alone, so a breaking vertical revision has no migration window. That gap also applies to every future vertical major.

Scope

  1. mortgage 2.0.0:

    • amount_currency (ISO 4217);
    • loan_amount, property_value, annual_income and cash_out_amount in whole units, each bounded;
    • all other fields unchanged.
  2. Frozen 1.0.0. The current file moves to verticals/archive/mortgage-1.0.0.json unchanged. verticals/mortgage.json becomes 2.0.0.

  3. Side-by-side rule (normative):

    • A receiver that advertises two versions of one vertical MUST validate each lead against the version its attributes.schema_version names.
    • It MUST refuse a version it does not advertise, with LCP-100, field = attributes.schema_version.
    • Pings validate the same way.
  4. Deprecation window: a receiver keeps 1.0.0 for at least 180 days after it first advertises 2.0.0.

Not in scope:

  • other verticals, which stay on their current versions;
  • core money fields;
  • converting the band enumerations;
  • moving property_address, which is the property and may differ from the consumer's location.

Proposed wire shape

Differences from 1.0.0:

1.0.0 2.0.0 Why
— amount_currency (string, ISO 4217, ^[A-Z]{3}$), required when any amount is present (dependentRequired) Amounts finally have a currency.
loan_amount_cents (integer, minimum: 0) loan_amount (integer, 1000–100000000) Whole units, bounded.
property_value_cents (integer, minimum: 0) property_value (integer, 1000–1000000000) Same reason.
annual_income_cents (integer, minimum: 0) annual_income (integer, 0–100000000) Same reason.
cash_out_amount_cents (integer, minimum: 0) cash_out_amount (integer, 0–100000000) Same reason.
schema_version const 1.0.0 const 2.0.0 Breaking for 1.0.0 senders.

Unchanged:

  • every other field, its x-lcp-pii class and its ping_safe flag;
  • the amounts stay restricted and ping_safe: false;
  • the platform-derived bands (loan_amount_band and the others) remain the only amount data a ping may carry. They are read in units of amount_currency.

Valid 1 — minimal lead

{ "vertical": "mortgage", "schema_version": "2.0.0" }

Valid 2 — refinance with amounts

{
  "vertical": "mortgage", "schema_version": "2.0.0", "amount_currency": "USD",
  "loan_type": "refinance", "loan_amount": 350000, "property_value": 500000,
  "annual_income": 120000, "cash_out_amount": 25000
}

Invalid A — amount without currency

"loan_amount": 350000 with no amount_currency is refused with LCP-100, field = attributes.amount_currency.

Invalid B — cents sent by mistake

"loan_amount": 35000000000 (meant as $350,000 in cents) is refused, because it is above 100000000.

Known limit: cents for loans of $1,000,000 or less pass the bound. Bands and buyer requirement profiles surface those as out-of-pattern.

Invalid C — 1.0.0 field on 2.0.0

"loan_amount_cents": 35000000 with schema_version 2.0.0 is refused, because additionalProperties is false.

Invalid D — unadvertised version

"schema_version": "1.0.0" sent to a receiver that advertises only 2.0.0 is refused with LCP-100, field = attributes.schema_version.

Privacy, security, and universal-core audit

  • No class changes. The amounts stay restricted and ping_safe: false. amount_currency is public and ping_safe: true, because a currency code identifies no one.
  • Universal core: untouched. amount_currency is not named currency, because core owns that name (SPEC §12). This follows LEP-0014.
  • The side-by-side rule narrows what a receiver accepts. It never widens it.

Compatibility, versioning, and deprecation impact

  • For mortgage senders: breaking, with a window of at least 180 days. Senders on 1.0.0 keep working at any receiver that still advertises it.
  • Normalisation is optional, and refused when the currency is unknown. A receiver MAY normalise 1.0.0 at intake (divide by 100). It MUST NOT assume a currency for this. It uses the sender's configured market currency, and refuses the normalisation when none is configured.
  • SDKs carry both model versions during the window. The 1.0.0 builder is marked deprecated.

SDK, OpenAPI, MCP, conformance, and operational impact

  • Schemas and SDKs: a mortgage 2.0.0 model in ten SDKs, with 1.0.0 kept and deprecated. Bundles and manifests carry both versions.
  • Reference platform: vertical validators keyed by (id, schema_version), loading verticals/archive/*.json, and capabilities that list both entries.

  • Conformance:

    • vectors for every Valid and Invalid example;
    • a side-by-side vector: the same receiver accepts 1.0.0 and 2.0.0, and refuses 9.9.9;
    • ping vectors for both versions.
  • OpenAPI and MCP: examples are regenerated on 2.0.0.
  • Metrics: per-sender counts of mortgage 1.0.0 traffic, to drive the end of the window.

Alternatives considered

  • Add amount_currency to 1.x as a minor change and keep cents. It fixes the currency but not the unit rule or the bounds, and it leaves mortgage inconsistent with LEP-0014. Rejected.
  • Convert in place without a version window. That breaks every shipped mortgage sender at once. Rejected.
  • Wait for a mortgage-specific defect (LEP-0014's deferred trigger). The missing currency is a defect by itself, and the side-by-side rule is needed before any vertical major can ship safely. Rejected.

Rollout, migration, observability, and rollback

  1. LCP: the side-by-side rule and the reference platform's versioned validators land first. That work is useful on its own.

  2. Next:

    • the 2.0.0 file lands;
    • 1.0.0 moves to verticals/archive/;
    • capabilities advertise both versions.
  3. Senders migrate in one edit: rename four fields, drop the ×100 conversion, add amount_currency and set schema_version to 2.0.0.
  4. End of window: a receiver stops advertising 1.0.0 once its per-sender counts reach zero, or after 180 days with notice.
  5. Rollback: a receiver keeps or restores the 1.0.0 advertisement. The archive file never changes.

Deferred questions

  • Bound values. 100000000 whole units suits USD, GBP and AUD markets but not high-denomination currencies such as JPY or KRW. Trigger: a receiver advertising mortgage in such a currency. The likely answer is bounds expressed per currency in the requirement profile.
  • Band labels per currency. The band enums read as US dollars (under_100k). Trigger: non-USD mortgage traffic.
  • A general archive policy for vertical majors. How long frozen files stay in the repository. Trigger: the third archived vertical version.
