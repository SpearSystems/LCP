LEP-0014 — personal_loans vertical 2.0.0: whole-unit applicant amounts and a regulated identity container

  • Title: personal_loans vertical 2.0.0: an interoperable personal-loan vertical with whole-unit applicant amounts and an LEP-0013 identity container
  • Author(s): LCP maintainers (draft from SpearPointX adopter evidence)
  • Date: 2026-11-19
  • Status: Review — public review open until 2026-12-03. Not accepted. A candidate implementation is on branch lep/0012-0013-sale-lifecycle; it does not merge until this record is Accepted (LEP.md stage 5).
  • Target version: v1.3.0 (a new vertical file is additive. The file's own schema_version is 2.0.0, so it can never be confused with the adopter's unpublished 1.0.0.)
  • Discussion: #256
  • Schema: verticals/personal_loans.json.
  • Depends on: LEP-0013 for the us_details container. If LEP-0013 is rejected, this vertical ships without us_details. Every other field stands alone.

Problem statement and evidence

No personal-loans vertical exists in LCP. The shipped verticals are home_services, insurance, legal, mortgage, mva and solar. SpearPointX registered its own personal_loans 1.0.0 to onboard a publisher, and published a schema URL under lcp.dev that LCP does not serve.

Unit conversion caused a real defect. Version 1.0.0 carries applicant-declared amounts as cents (loan_amount_cents, monthly_income_cents). Applicants type whole dollars, so every integration has to multiply by 100. In practice that failed in three ways:

  • The adopter's own integration guide multiplied twice. A $1,000 request went out as $100,000, and the schema accepted it.
  • No-code form tools (GoHighLevel, Heyflow) cannot multiply a field value. The guide had to send those publishers through a separate automation service just to add two zeros.
  • The buyer API the adopter delivers to takes whole dollars. Its adapter divides by 100 again.

The 1.0.0 identity container cannot validate. Its us_details lists ssn, license_number and routing_number as required but declares no such properties (see LEP-0013, evidence item 2).

Scope

  • Vertical (additive): a new verticals/personal_loans.json at schema_version 2.0.0, as proposed in the linked schema.
  • Vertical-authoring rule (documentation, docs/VERTICALS.md or equivalent): applicant-declared amounts may be whole units of a declared vertical attribute such as amount_currency, named without a _cents suffix and bounded. The attribute is not named currency: core schemas own that name (SPEC §12, tools/check_universal_core.py). Platform-priced money stays integer minor units with _cents.

  • Not in scope:

    • changing existing verticals (mortgage.loan_amount_cents and others);
    • core money fields;
    • non-US personal loans.

Proposed wire shape

The full schema is in the linked file. Differences from the adopter's 1.0.0:

1.0.0 2.0.0 Why
loan_amount_cents (integer, multiple of 100) loan_amount (integer, 100–50000, whole units) The form value passes straight through, with no conversion step. The bound rejects most wrong-unit values.
monthly_income_cents (integer, multiple of 100) monthly_income (integer, 0–50000, whole units) Same reason.
account_number at top level us_details.account_number, regulated Account and routing numbers together are LEP-0013 bank_account identifiers and sit in one container.
us_details classed contact; ssn, license_number, routing_number required but undeclared us_details classed restricted; all five identifiers declared with patterns and x-lcp-regulated; only country_code required Fixes the unvalidatable container and aligns it with LEP-0013.
20 required fields 4 required: vertical, schema_version, amount_currency, loan_amount What a given buyer needs belongs in its requirement profile (regulated_fields and the existing predicates), not in the interoperable schema.
street_address in attributes core location.street_address SPEC §3: a vertical MUST NOT redefine a core field name (tools/check_reserved_fields.py).
schema_version 1.0.0 2.0.0 A breaking change for 1.0.0 senders.

Unchanged:

  • every other field, its class and its ping_safe flag;
  • the platform-derived bands (loan_amount_band, monthly_income_band), which remain the only amount data a ping may carry.

The rule a publisher reads. Type what the applicant typed. 1000 means $1,000. Prices and payouts the platform reports back stay in cents (payout_cents, LEP-0012), because machines produce those.

Valid 1 — minimal lead

{ "vertical": "personal_loans", "schema_version": "2.0.0", "amount_currency": "USD", "loan_amount": 1000 }

Valid 2 — full lead with regulated identifiers

See LEP-0013 Valid 1.

Invalid A — cents sent by mistake

"loan_amount": 100000 (meant as $1,000 in cents) is refused with LCP-100, field = attributes.loan_amount, because it exceeds 50000.

Known limit: cents for requests of $500 or less (50000 and below) pass the bound. Requirement profiles and the platform's band derivation surface those as out-of-pattern requests. Bounds alone cannot close the gap.

Invalid B — float amount

"loan_amount": 1000.5 is refused, because the type is integer.

Invalid C — 1.0.0 field name on 2.0.0

"loan_amount_cents": 100000 with schema_version 2.0.0 is refused, because additionalProperties is false.

Privacy, security, and universal-core audit

  • Classes are carried over from 1.0.0 field by field, with no downgrade. us_details moves from contact to restricted, which is more protective and matches LEP-0011's container rule: a container takes the maximum of its children.
  • Regulated identifiers follow LEP-0013 in full. Without that record accepted, us_details is removed from this file.

  • Ping: only public, ping_safe: true fields:

    • bands;
    • loan_purpose, income_source, income_frequency;
    • direct_deposit, owns_vehicle, is_homeowner.
  • Universal core: untouched. Everything here is in attributes.

Compatibility, versioning, and deprecation impact

  • LCP release: additive (a new vertical file).
  • For the adopter's 1.0.0 senders: breaking. The migration is below.
  • Precedent. Whole-unit applicant amounts differ from mortgage's loan_amount_cents. The vertical-authoring rule makes this an explicit, per-vertical choice. Converting other verticals is a deferred question, not part of this record.

SDK, OpenAPI, MCP, conformance, and operational impact

  • Schemas and SDKs: new vertical models in ten SDKs, with bundles and manifests regenerated. A personal_loans builder takes whole units and never multiplies.
  • Conformance: test-vectors/verticals/personal_loans.json covers every Valid and Invalid example, ping eligibility per field, and the us_details gate vectors (LEP-0013).
  • OpenAPI and MCP: vertical examples are regenerated with reserved test identifiers only.

Alternatives considered

  • Keep cents and add bounds. This catches the double conversion but keeps the multiply step that no-code publishers cannot do and that buyers undo. Rejected.
  • Decimal strings ("1000.00"). These parse in every stack, but publishers still have to format them, and they add a third numeric form to the protocol. Rejected.
  • A _dollars suffix. It is currency-specific, so a future GBP or AUD personal-loans market would carry a lie in the field name. Whole units of the declared amount_currency stay correct everywhere. Rejected.
  • Keep the 20 required fields. That encodes one buyer's requirements as everyone's schema. Rejected, in favour of requirement profiles.

Rollout, migration, observability, and rollback

  1. LCP: the vertical lands with LEP-0013's gate. If LEP-0013 has not been accepted, it lands without us_details.

  2. Adopter migration (SpearPointX):

    • accept schema_version 1.0.0 and 2.0.0 side by side for 90 days;
    • normalise 1.0.0 at intake (loan_amount = loan_amount_cents / 100);
    • log per-sender 1.0.0 counts;
    • remove 1.0.0 once the counts reach zero.
  3. Publisher change: a publisher moves in one edit. Rename two fields, drop the ×100 conversion, move account_number into us_details, and set schema_version to 2.0.0.
  4. Rollback: the adopter keeps accepting 1.0.0. The LCP file is additive and can be withdrawn before release without affecting other verticals.

Deferred questions

  • Whole-unit amounts in other verticals (mortgage.loan_amount_cents, property_value_cents). Trigger: a no-code integration defect or support burden in another vertical, or a second adopter asking.
  • Bound values. 50000 is set for short-term and mid-size installment loans. Trigger: a buyer with larger products, or refusal metrics showing legitimate requests above the bound.
  • Non-US personal loans. Trigger: buyer evidence outside the US. That needs a country container and, for identifiers, its own LEP-0013 successor.
