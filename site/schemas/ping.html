For exclusivity 'exclusive' this MUST be 1 — the one-slot special case (enforced by the top-level conditional in this schema). Absent = the platform does not advertise slot state (v1.0 behavior). A platform MUST NOT send slots_available: 0; zero remaining means stop pinging. Non-PII integer count carrying no consumer data: it joins the ping strict allowlist as a reviewed non-PII field, and conformance keeps rejecting every other field outside the allowlist (LCP-008 PII_IN_PING protection preserved) and every attribute not tagged ping_safe: true in the vertical schema.
