LCP Bid Message

https://lcp.dev/schemas/bid.json · Buyer's response to a ping. Contains the bid price, accept/reject decision, and estimated contact speed. The platform collects bids from all pinged buyers and selects the winner. Created by Spear Systems (a Spear company). Open standard — Apache 2.0, free to implement.

It replaces bid_price_cents on this bid (the token carries the agreed price); a sender presents it only toward a receiver advertising `negotiation` and a parsed lcp_versions element of 1.2.0 or later.
ping_idstringinternalyesID of the ping being responded to.
reject_reasonstringinternalWhy the buyer rejected (e.g. out_of_hours, capacity_full, vertical_mismatch, price_too_low). Open enumeration. Optional.
slots_claimedintegerinternalv1.1 (LEP-0003 §2): buyer slots this buyer claims if selected. The claim is a request, not a reservation — the platform awards slots at post time (delivery is the claim event; bids are non-binding). Only meaningful with decision 'accept'; absent = 1. Capped at exactly 1 for v1.1: multi-slot agency claims (N > 1) have no delivery path on the per-(lead, offer) award guard and no awarded-count wire signal, so they are deferred (LEP-0003 Deferred questions, #153 T5) — this schema enforces maximum: 1 now. Capability-gated (SPEC.md §8): senders MUST NOT include v1.1 slot fields toward receivers whose lcp_versions advertisement does not parse to major == 1, minor >= 1.
