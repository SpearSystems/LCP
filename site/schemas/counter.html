LCP Counter Message

https://lcp.dev/schemas/counter.json · Either party answers the standing negotiation message (LEP-0009). Strict allowlist: integer minor units, ISO 4217 currency, opaque ids, round counters, and closed/known reason values only. No consumer PII and no free text. Created by Spear Systems (a Spear company). Open standard — Apache 2.0, free to implement.

negotiation_idstringinternalyesMUST name an existing negotiation for this (tenant, buyer, ping) triple; otherwise unknown_negotiation (or LCP-002 when it belongs to another buyer/tenant).
offer_idstringinternalyesMUST equal the negotiation's offer (LCP-100 on mismatch).
ping_idstringinternalyesMUST equal the negotiation's ping (LCP-100 on mismatch).
price_centsintegerinternalRequired unless decision = refuse. counter: subject to the monotone rule and at or above floor_price_cents. accept: MUST equal the price of the message it answers.
rejection_reasonstringinternalOpen enumeration. Required when decision = refuse; absent otherwise. Known values: below_floor_price, non_monotone_counter, round_limit_exceeded, negotiation_expired, unknown_negotiation, negotiation_closed.
rounds_remainingintegerinternalyesThe sender's view of the buyer counters still available at the moment this message is sent, before it is applied. A stale value is LCP-100.
