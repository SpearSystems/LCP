LCP Quote Message

https://lcp.dev/schemas/quote.json · Platform-opened, bounded price negotiation for a named ping (LEP-0009). Ping-class: integer minor units, ISO 4217 currency, opaque ids, timestamps, round counters, and closed/known reason values only. No consumer PII and no free text — the strict allowlist is the enforcement. Created by Spear Systems (a Spear company). Open standard — Apache 2.0, free to implement.

Top-level properties

PropertyTypePII classRequiredDescription
currencyref core.json#/$defs/currency—yes
expires_atstringinternalyesAbsolute end of the negotiation. MUST NOT be later than the ping's expires_at (or its timestamp plus TTL).
floor_price_centsintegerinternalyesRestated from the ping so the buyer can verify the floor and the monotone rule without re-reading the ping.
max_roundsintegerinternalRounds this negotiation may run. Absent = 3 (D8 default); above the hard cap of 5 is LCP-100, never a deployment knob.
negotiation_idstringinternalyesOpaque platform-assigned negotiation id, unique per negotiation. The only lookup key a later message may use.
offer_idstringinternalyesThe buyer offer being priced; MUST equal the ping's offer_id (LCP-100 on mismatch).
ping_idstringinternalyesThe ping this negotiation prices. Supplies the floor, currency, and the TTL ceiling.
price_centsintegerinternalThe ask in minor units of currency. MUST be at or above floor_price_cents; below floor is refused below_floor_price and never clamped.
rejection_reasonstringinternalOpen enumeration. Present only on a terminal refusal message (no negotiation opened): below_floor_price, round_limit_exceeded, negotiation_expired, unknown_negotiation, negotiation_closed. When present, price_cents MUST be absent.
rounds_remainingintegerinternalyesBuyer counters still available at the moment this message is sent. On the opening quote this equals the effective max_rounds (a quote consumes no round).
