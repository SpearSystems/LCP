Each entry is HMAC-SHA256(pair_secret, contact-preimage) — lowercase 64-hex, the same form as phone_hash (SPEC.md §9); the unsalted SHA-1-shaped digest of the reference network is explicitly NOT adopted (enumerable keyspaces invert it; §14 blocker B3). The platform matches digests only against values it holds in plaintext for this pair and cannot verify reported digests without the plaintext — the trust model is format + count sanity plus authenticated-sender attestation, scoped to one lead_id. Keys MUST follow {org}.{division}.{purpose} - at least three lowercase dot-separated segments - and lcp.core.* is reserved for the core. Values stay deployment-defined: the core does not and cannot validate namespaced payloads. Shape authority for a registered namespace is its record (LEP-0006 section B.2 defines {org}.form.questions: at most 25 entries of question_id/question_text/answer_text, question_text at most 300 and answer_text at most 1000 characters). These are receiver rules, not schema rules: a receiver that implements a registered form.questions namespace MUST validate entries and accept or reject the message (LCP-100), while receivers that do not recognize a namespace ignore its value. Bound hierarchy (LEP-0006 section B.2): the registered form.questions shape ceiling is 133,140 B worst-case UTF-8, receivers SHOULD reject extension totals above the 32 KiB soft policy target (a fully shape-conformant sender can exceed it; issue #197 tracks the re-tune), and the operative hard bound is the transport body cap (max_body_bytes, default 2,000,000 B). Never ping_safe: this field exists only on lead and post - ping/bid/ack keep their strict allowlists - and a platform MUST NOT derive ping attributes, compliance_flags, or any other ping field from extension content. Extension content is lead/post PII-scope content: it inherits section 9 retention and erasure, and question/answer text MUST NOT carry contact values, government identifiers, payment credentials, consent tokens, or evidence values (those have structured homes in consumer, compliance.consent_evidence[], and attachments[]).
