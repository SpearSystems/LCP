LCP Personal Loans Vertical Schema

https://lcp.dev/verticals/personal_loans.json · Short-term and installment personal loan lead attributes (LEP-0014). Every field is tagged ping_safe: true|false; the conformance runner rejects any ping containing a non-ping_safe field. Applicant-declared amounts are whole units of amount_currency; regulated identifiers in us_details follow LEP-0013. Buyers require further fields through requirement profiles, not this schema. Created by Spear Systems (a Spear company). Open standard - Apache 2.0, free to implement.
