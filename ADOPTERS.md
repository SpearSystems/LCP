## How to Add Your Organization

1. Fork the repo.
2. Add a row to the table above with your organization name, use case,
   date, and a public contact (email, GitHub handle, or website).
3. Open a pull request.

Adoption claims should be publicly verifiable (a URL, product page, or
open-source repo demonstrating LCP usage).
